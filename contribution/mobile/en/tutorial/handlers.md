# Handlers

## What does a Handler do?
A Handler is an experienced player who guides eligible newcomers through early progression. Once you reach level `200`, newcomers can choose you as their Handler from your profile. You cannot assign a runner to yourself.

## Who can choose me as their Handler?
- The runner must be between level `15` and level `30`.
- The runner's account must be no more than `60` days old.
- You must be at least level `200`.
- The system checks the runner's alt accounts and other eligibility rules at selection and again at level `50`. Rewards are not guaranteed.
- Neither the runner nor the Handler can have other accounts. If either does, the mentorship automatically becomes ineligible, and no Handler reward or mentorship point is granted.

## How does a runner choose me?
1. The runner opens your player profile.
2. They select **Choose as Handler**.
3. They read the permanent-choice warning, tick the acknowledgement box, and select **Choose permanently**.

A runner can choose only one Handler. Their choice takes effect immediately and is permanent; you do not need to accept it.

## How do I manage my runners?
Your assigned runners are listed under **Profile → Assets** and are visible only to you. You cannot revoke their mentorship. Before reaching level `50`, a runner can end it from their own **Profile → Assets**. This permanently closes the mentorship, and they can never choose another Handler.

A runner's chosen Handler appears under their **Profile → Assets** by default, but they can hide it in **Settings**. Your total **Runners Mentored** count remains public.

## When do I receive rewards?
When an eligible runner reaches level `50`, the system checks the mentorship again. If all checks pass, the mentorship is added to your public total and you receive the current Handler reward. View the current reward under **Profile → Assets**.
