# Community Raffles

**Community Raffles** are free, player-run giveaways. An organizer publishes a raffle, players join before the deadline, and the game randomly selects an eligible winner when the organizer closes it.

The organizer is responsible for delivering any promised prize. The game does not hold, transfer, or guarantee prizes.

## Open Community Raffles

- Open **Apps**, then select **Community Raffles**.
- You can also use the raffle-drum icon in the Global Chat header.

## Join a Raffle

1. Open a raffle and read its title, description, and any equipment snapshots.
2. Check the minimum level and deadline.
3. Select **Join raffle** before the deadline.

Joining is free, but it cannot be undone. You cannot leave a raffle or enter it twice.

You cannot join your own raffle. Your account must meet the minimum level and have no account restrictions. If one of your associated accounts has joined, your other associated accounts cannot join the same raffle.

## Create a Raffle

You must be level 300, have no account restrictions, and own one **Community Raffle Kit**.

When creating a raffle, you choose:

- A title and optional description
- A duration of 1, 3, 7, 14, or 30 days
- A minimum entry level from 50 to 300
- Up to three optional equipment snapshots

The title and description must be relevant and follow the game rules. They are checked before publishing.

Publishing consumes one Community Raffle Kit. Published settings cannot be edited, and deleting the raffle does not refund the Kit.

## Equipment Snapshots and Prizes

Attached equipment is shown as a snapshot for players to inspect. Attaching equipment does not remove it from the organizer, reserve it for the winner, or make the game deliver it automatically.

The organizer must clearly explain the prize and deliver it to the winner. Only join raffles whose terms you understand.

## Close a Raffle

After the entry deadline, the organizer has seven days to select **Close raffle**. The game then randomly selects one entrant who is still eligible.

The winner and organizer receive mail, and the result is announced in Global Chat. If no eligible entrant remains, the raffle closes without a winner.

If the organizer does not close the raffle within seven days after the deadline, it becomes abandoned and no winner can be selected.

## Delete a Raffle

The organizer can permanently delete their raffle at any time. Deletion cannot be undone and does not refund the Community Raffle Kit.

Moderators may also delete raffles that break the game rules.
