## What be Pets?

Pets be virtual companions that live in yer apartment. They be needin' regular attention to stay healthy and happy, matey.

## How do I adopt a pet?

Ye be needin' an **Apartment** 'n a **Sanctuary Adoption Pass** t' adopt a pet, aye.

1. Obtain a **Sanctuary Adoption Pass** by craftin' it wit' Volunteer Service Medallions (earned from th' Pet Shelter Volunteer Shift AFK action)
2. Visit **Neon Paws Sanctuary** (th' Pet Shop NPC)
3. Activate the **Pet Adoption Buff** which lets all scallywags adopt durin' its duration
4. Tap **Adopt a new pet** while th' buff be active

> **Note:** There be a cooldown period between adoptions, ye landlubber.

## How many pets can I have, matey?

Th' number o' pets ye can keep depends on yer apartment type:

- Small Apartment: 1 pet
- Medium Apartment: 2 pets
- Large Apartment: 3 pets
- Penthouse: 4 pets

Pets exceedin' yer apartment capacity will be "livin' on the street" an' can nah be interacted wit' until ye upgrade yer apartment, matey.

## How do I take care o' me pets?

Pets 'ave four stats that need regular attention. Neglected pets lose health 'n may die, arr:

- **Hunger**: Feed usin' **ElectroFiber Bites**
- **Cleanliness**: Clean usin' **FurFlush Spray**
- **Happiness**: Play usin' **NanoNibble Ball**
- **Health**: Heal usin' **PawfectCure** when sick

Yer pet'll show status indicators like Hungry, Dirty, Unhappy, orr Sick when care be needed. Well-kept pets (all stats healthy) will slowly regenerate health.

> **Tip:** Check on yer pets daily t' keep 'em healthy, ye scurvy dog. Buy all pet care items at Neon Paws Sanctuary.

## What happens if me pet's health reaches zero?

When a pet's health drops t' zero, it becomes **Deceased**. Ye 'ave a limited time t' revive yer pet:

1. Visit **Neon Paws Sanctuary**
2. Activate the **Pet Revive Buff**
3. Use th' revive option on yer deceased pet durin' th' buff window

Deceased pets be automatically removed aft a week if nah revived, arr.

## Can I customize me pet?

- **Rename**: Change yer pet's name (4-16 characters, no spaces)
- **Reroll Appearance**: Roll a new random appearance (Dog or Cat)

## Where can I find me pets?

Access yer pets through yer **Apartment** or th' **Pet List** menu. Tap any pet t' view stats 'n interact wit' it, matey.
