## What be th' Furniture System?

Th' Furniture System be a late game collectible system that provides minor stat bonuses t' th' scallywag. It allows level 300+ pirates t' craft 'n install furniture in thar apartments that provides permanent stat bonuses. T' receive bonus stats from th' furniture, ye need t' activate 'em usin' `Energy Cells`.

## How many furniture slots do I 'ave, matey?

Th' number o' furniture slots be based on th' apartment tier.
Basically, th' higher th' apartment tier, th' more furniture slots ye 'ave, matey.
Fer more info, see apartment details, matey.

## How do I get furniture materials, arr?

Complete AFK tasks in `Crystal Sands` (level 300+ required) t' gather materials, arr:

`Salvage Beached Tech Cargo` (Primary materials source)

- Costs `AI Cores` and `Tech Scrap`
- Rewards: `Synthetic Polymer`, `Alloy Frame`, `Smart Fabric`

`Salvage Power Units` (Energy Cells for activation)

- Costs `AI Cores` and `Tech Scrap`
- Rewards: `Energy Cells` (primary), wit' small chance fer craftin' materials

## How do I craft furniture?

Furniture craftin' uses a progressive `Assembly Kit` system:

1. `Craft Basic Furniture Assembly Kit`

   - Requires: `Synthetic Polymer`, `Alloy Frame`, `Smart Fabric`
   - Level required: 300

2. `Upgrade to Advanced Kit`

   - Requires: Multiple `Basic Kits`
   - Level required: 400

3. `Upgrade to High-End Kit`

   - Requires: Multiple `Advanced Kits`
   - Level required: 500

4. **Trade kit fer furniture**
   - Visit the `Furniture Vendor NPC` in `Crystal Sands`
   - Trade 1 kit fer any furniture in that tier
   - Choose the specific furniture ye want

> **Note:** Ye must craft kits first, then trade 'em wit' th' Furniture Vendor. Ye can nah craft furniture directly, scallywag.

## I already traded me kit fer furniture, why am I not gettin' the stat bonuses?

Thar be a few possible reasons:

- Ye 'ave th' furniture but 'ave not placed it in yer apartment yet.
- Ye 'ave placed th' furniture in yer apartment but 'ave not activated it yet; only activated furniture be able t' provide stat bonuses.

## How do I activate furniture?

1. Ensure ye 'ave enough `Energy Cells` in yer inventory
2. Open yer `Furniture page` from yer apartment
3. Select th' furniture ye want t' activate, assign th' furniture if ye 'ave not already done so.
4. Click th' `Activate` button
5. Furniture automatically enables if slots be available

> **Note:** Activated furniture requires periodic reactivation, aye.

## How do I disable a furniture?

Ye can simply remove th' furniture from yer apartment. Ye'll still own th' furniture, but it'll nah provide stat bonuses.

## What happens if I downgraded me apartment t' fewer slots than I 'ave furniture?

When ye leave yer current apartment, all furniture slots will be cleared, so when ye rent a new apartment, ye will need to assign the furniture to the new apartment.

## Can I use furniture without an apartment, matey?

Nay, matey. Furniture bonuses only apply when ye 'ave an active apartment rental. If yer apartment rental expires:

- Yer furniture ownership be preserved
- Activated furniture continues countin' down
- Ye receive zero stat bonuses until ye rent another apartment and assign the furniture to the new apartment.

## Wha' happens when furniture expires, arr?

When th' activation time expires:

- Furniture be automatically disabled 'n stops providin' bonuses
- Furniture remains in yer inventory 'n stays assigned t' th' apartment slot.
- Ye may activate them at any time again, arr.

## Can I stack multiple furniture from th' same category, matey?

Aye, matey. Ye can activate multiple furniture from th' same category t' stack bonuses.

## Can I own th' same furniture multiple times, arr?

Nay. Each specific furniture type can only be owned **once per pirate**. Ye cannot trade fer duplicate furniture.

**Example:** Ye can own one `Neural Interface Desk` (Tier 1 Study), but ye can also own `Holographic Terminal` (Tier 2 Study) since they be different furniture types.

## What be the tier differences?

All three tiers within each category provide **identical stat bonuses**, aye.

- **Tier 1** (Level 300+)
- **Tier 2** (Level 400+)
- **Tier 3** (Level 500+)

Choose based on yer preference or level availability, not power level, scallywag.

## How much does th' furniture system cost, matey?

**Material Gatherin'**:

- Requires multiple AFK task runs t' gather materials, matey
- Costs `AI Cores` and `Tech Scrap` per run
- `BTC` required fer craftin' Assembly Kits

**Furniture Activation** (periodic maintenance):

- Costs `Energy Cells` per furniture
- Requires regular AFK task runs t' farm Energy Cells, arr

## Where can I access th' furniture system, arr?

- `Crystal Sands` - Location fer AFK tasks and `Furniture Vendor NPC` (level 300+ required)
- `Apartment page` - Access th' furniture management interface
- `Craftin' menu` - Craft Assembly Kits
