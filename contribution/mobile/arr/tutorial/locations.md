## Locations
Th' game begins in Shangri-La City Centerr. From thar, ye can explore various areas by goin' t' different subway stations. 

## City Map

![Map](/resources/mobile-tutorial/Map.png)

## Shangri-La City Centerrr  
![Shangri-La City Center](/resources/mobile-tutorial/Shangri-LaCityCenter.png)  
'Tis th' Main HUB t' which ye will often return t' aft ye die, t' print equipment, t' make purchases 'n fer various other activities.

## 3D Molecularr Printerrr 
![3D Molecular Printer](/resources/mobile-tutorial/Molecular3DPrinter.png)  
In th' City Centerr, ye can find th' Molecular 3D Printer, which be used t' print yer equipment from various caches.

Skewerin' on th' printer option will bring ye t' th' printin' panel which lists all th' caches ye 'ave.
'T costs BitCoin t' print, so make sure ye 'ave enough. All details includin' BTC cost 'n printin' experience given will be listed on th' cache printin' panel. 
If ye wish t' print a cache, all ye needs t' do be t' jab on th' cache ye wish t' print. If ye 'ave multiple caches o' th' same quality 'n type, 'twill bring up a dialog askin' how many ye wants t' print.  

A good strategy be t' print all th' trash items first then th' common, savin' rare, legendary 'n epic fer later levels. Th' reason be that printin' trash 'n common quality items first provides ye wit' printin' experience; these items can then be scrapped t' acquire Tech Scraps, which can also boost yer Printin' Level or be used t' craft other items 'n unlock locked containers.

## Weapon Fettlin'  
![Weaponsmith](/resources/mobile-tutorial/WeaponSmith.png)  
Th' Weapon Fettlin' be a vendor in Shangri-La City Centerr who 'as items fer sale 'n allows ye t' sell yer items fer BTC. Ye will see th' items available fer sale at th' top. Ye can skewer on any o' 'em 'n choose Buy if ye can afford 'em.  If ye 'ave equipment wit' th' **bargain** stat, ye'll get a better deal. 

If ye want t' sell somethin' t' th' Weaponsmith, ye'll need t' open yer loot, skewer on any item ye wish t' sell 'n choose th' Sell option. Fer some items, it may be more worth it t' sell 'em at th' player market.

Ye can repair yer items here too. Ye lose durability th' more ye use yer equipment. 'T costs mighty wee t' repair 'em t' mint condition, so be sure t' repair frequently as needed.

## Trinoky Mart  
![Trinoky Mart](/resources/mobile-tutorial/TrinokyMart.png)  
Here ye can buy medical items wit' BTC. Prices be completely separate from th' player market, so be sure t' check both! 

## Terminal 
![Terminal](/resources/mobile-tutorial/Terminal.png)  
Th' Terminal be used t' merge key fragments int' secret keys t' unlock higher-level train stations.

Here ye can also unlock locked containers (found in dungeons) wit' th' Tech Scrap that ye 'ave.

## Bank o' Arasaka
![Bank of Arasaka](/resources/mobile-tutorial/BankOfArasaka.png)  
In addition t' yer in'entory, each player also 'as access t' a vault in th' Shangri-La State Bank.

Initially ye 'ave 6 bank slots. Ye can choose t' permanently expand slots by payin' BTC. Keep in mind that th' cost o' th' slots ye buy will increase wit' each purchase.  

Items that be stackable will occupy only one bank slot jus' like in yer in'entory. Each deposit will cost 100 BTC, but takin' somethin' out be free o' charge. 

## Hyperr Train Central Station  
![Hyper Train Station](/resources/mobile-tutorial/HyperTrainCentralStation.png)  
'Tis where yer adventure begins. Th' train can take ye t' multiple stations. Each station 'as three areas full o' enemies o' different levels. 'Tis also where ye will find dungeons. Gather 3 unique key fragments from the dungeons t' unlock the next train station. 

In each station, ye can also use yer AI Cores t' farm EXP 'n scavenge fer materials that are needed fer craftin' healin' items 'n ammo.

## Shangri-La Commercial Area (Job Hub)  
![Commercial Area](/resources/mobile-tutorial/Shangri-LaCommercialArea.png)  
At this location, ye can look fer jobs t' increase yer printin' level, ammo/medical craftin' levels, 'n minin' level. Each job (AFK activity) shows wha' ye can accomplish wit' it.

## Shangri-La Market Street  
![Street Market](/resources/mobile-tutorial/Shangri-LaMarketStreet.png)  
Here ye can sell items t' other crewmates 'n purchase items from 'em within th' game fer BTC.  

Bear in mind that whenever ye want t' sell or buy an item on th' market, ye'll 'ave t' complete a captcha o' 4 numbers.  'Tis in place t' prevent bots from usin' 'n manipulatin' th' crewmate market.

## Cosmetic Cyberwearrr  
![Cosmetic Cyberwear](/resources/mobile-tutorial/CosmeticCyberwear.png)  
In this location ye can buy in-game subscriptions fer real doubloons.

These subscriptions help maintain th' cost o' th' game 'n allow ye, dependin' on th' type o' subscription, t' 'ave a coloured chat border, a background animation fer yer profile, a title 'n th' ability t' upload a custom profile picture.

Bear in mind that ye be nah forced t' purchase these subscriptions at all 'n **these subscriptions offer no P2W benefits**. 
Th' subscription benefits be purely cosmetic.  
Ye can still play the game without subscribin'.

## Arasaka Unit Exchange
![Arasaka Exchange](/resources/mobile-tutorial/ArasakaUnitExchange.png)  
Here ye can buy Transmitters that 'ave various effects. These Transmitters be purchasable only wit' Units (premium currency).  

Bear in mind that if one scallywag activates a Transmitter, every player on th' server will benefit from it.
**They be nah P2W.** 
They can nah offer an advantage t' only one player. All hands benefit from th' effects.

Here ye can also purchase Units, th' in-game premium currency, wit' real doubloons.
In th' Android/Apple application, th' prices o' Units will appear in yer local currency.  
In th' browser version (both mobile or desktop views), th' prices will appear in USD only.
