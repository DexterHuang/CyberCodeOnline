# Guides

## What does a Guide do?
A Guide be an experienced player who steers eligible deckhands through their early voyage. Once ye reach level `200`, deckhands can choose ye as their Guide from yer profile. Ye cannot assign a deckhand to yerself.

## Who can choose me as their Guide?
- The deckhand must be between level `15` and level `30`.
- The deckhand's account must be no older than `60` days.
- Ye must be at least level `200`.
- The system checks the deckhand's alt accounts and other rules at selection and again at level `50`. Rewards be never guaranteed.
- Neither the deckhand nor the Guide can have other accounts. If either does, the guidin' automatically be ineligible, and no Guide reward or guidin' point be granted.

## How does a deckhand choose me?
1. The deckhand opens yer player profile.
2. They select **Choose as Guide**.
3. They read the permanent-choice warnin', tick the acknowledgement box, and select **Choose permanently**.

A deckhand can choose only one Guide. Their choice takes hold at once and lasts forever; ye need not accept it.

## How do I manage me deckhands?
Yer assigned deckhands be listed under **Profile → Assets** and visible only to ye. Ye cannot end their guidin'. Before reachin' level `50`, a deckhand can end it from their own **Profile → Assets**. This closes the guidin' forever, and they can never choose another Guide.

A deckhand's chosen Guide shows under their **Profile → Assets** by default, but they can hide it in **Settings**. Yer total **Deckhands Guided** count stays public.

## When do I receive rewards?
When an eligible deckhand reaches level `50`, the system checks the guidin' again. If all checks pass, the guidin' joins yer public total and ye receive the current Guide reward. See the current reward under **Profile → Assets**.
