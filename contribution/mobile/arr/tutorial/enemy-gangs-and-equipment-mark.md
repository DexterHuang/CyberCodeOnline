# Enemy Gangs & Mark

![EnemyGangGraph](/resources/mobile-tutorial/EnemyGangGraph.png)

All enemies 'ave a gang they belong t', as each area will 'ave a different gang _'dominant'_ in retrospect. These gangs affect how players approach, as th' enemy gang can either benefit players or make th' fight even harder.

In summary, enemy gang names 'n traits be as follows:
- **Animals:** Increased damage t' **Disruptor** players (up t' 10%)

- **Voodoo Boys**: Increased damage t' **Makeshift** players (up t' 10%)

- **Scavengers**: Increased damage t' **Lethal** players (up t' 10%)

![EnemyGang](/resources/mobile-tutorial/EnemyGang.png)

One o' th' few ways t' know which gangs be dominatin' th' area be through **Nearby Enemies** or surfin' around **Dungeon**. By seein' thar gang name on th' top o' its health bar, ye can notify who's who mighty handsomely. Players can still change thar gear in-fight stance if they reckon th' enemies will benefit from thar **Mark** spots.

## Equipment Mark

All gear / equipment (except backpacks & modules) 'as a **Mark** attached t' it, wit' a different effect dependin' on th' enemy's influence.

In summary, marks' names 'n traits be as follows:

- **Lethal**: Increase damage t' **Animals** enemies (up t' 10%)

- **Disruptor**: Increase damage t' **Voodoo Boys** enemies (up t' 10%)

- **Makeshift**: Increase damage t' **Scavengers** enemies (up t' 10%)

Keep in mind that all gear gained via print or drop, either afore or aft **2022/01/05 update**, will 'ave a random Mark automatically attached t' it.

## Knowin' Yer Designated Mark

![EnemyGangMark](/resources/mobile-tutorial/EnemyGangMark.png)

Every player 'as a different **Mark**, determined by th' majority o' gear Marks in yer arsenal (ye can see yer designated **Mark** at th' **Equipped** section on th' **Loot** tab). Fer example, if yer equipped gear we be dominated by **Makeshift** mark, then Makeshift be yer classified Mark.

![EnemyGangStats](/resources/mobile-tutorial/EnemyGangStats.png)

Ye can see th' effects o' havin' a certain amount o' **Mark** by tappin' th' yellow Mark text on th' Equipped section or from th' Player's stats on th' **Profile** tab. As th' graph above suggests, every Mark will affect both gangs in a good or bad way.
