## Plunderin' Gear Exchange

Arrr, th' Gear Exchange lets ye trade yer unwanted EPIC loot fer bountiful booty! By handin' over four eligible EPIC treasures, ye'll get a random haul o' Epic Cache Fragments; th' odds o' yer reward be shown in th' game's lookin' glass an' may change wit' th' tides.

No buff or special ritual be needed fer equipment extraction—ye can swap yer unwanted EPIC loot any time, matey! Only unlocked, non-backpack EPIC treasures in yer inventory (not stashed in th' vault) can be picked.

## What Be th' Gear Exchange?

Th' Gear Exchange lets pirates trade unwanted EPIC loot fer useful booty. By exchangin' four eligible EPIC treasures, ye get a random haul o' Epic Cache Fragments; th' odds be shown in th' game UI an' may shift like the sea.

## Why Bother Wit' Gear Exchange?

- Excellent fer clearin' out yer hold—turn many EPIC treasures int' compact fragments
- Clears unused EPIC loot from yer inventory
- Nets ye Epic Cache Fragments that can be fused int' guaranteed EPIC booty

## How t' Board th' Gear Exchange

Seek out th' Equipment Exchange NPC, matey! Th' feature be open t' all pirates, no gang required.

## Swappin' Epic Loot fer Epic Cache Fragments

1. Open th' Gear Exchange page, matey.
2. Pick four unlocked, non-backpack EPIC treasures from yer inventory.
3. Confirm th' trade t' claim yer reward—ye'll get around 4 Epic Cache Fragments, dependin' on th' whims o' fate. Fer th' exact odds, check th' game UI.

**No buff or activation required**—ye can extract equipment any time, arrr!

## Fusin' Epic Cache Fragments Into Epic Gear Caches

1. Gather up eight Epic Cache Fragments (plundered from gear exchange).
2. Activate the `Epic Fragment Fusion Access` buff (find it in th' shop or from global buffs).
3. Navigate t' th' Fragment Fusion page.
4. Confirm t' fuse fragments int' one Epic Equipment Cache wit' guaranteed EPIC booty inside!

**Note**: Fragment fusion needs th' `Epic Fragment Fusion Access` buff t' be active. Th' buff 'as a cooldown—ye can only fuse once per activation.

## Who Can Trade in th' Epic Gear Exchange?

- Item must be EPIC tier, arrr!
- Item can't be a backpack, matey.
- Item must be unlocked, matey.
- Item must be in yer inventory (not the vault, ye landlubber).
